Buildexa Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

Buildexa ("the app", "we") is a construction calculator and cost estimator. This policy explains what information the app handles. We respect your privacy and keep things simple: we do not require an account, and we do not run our own servers that collect your data.

1. Information stored on your device

Your projects, calculations, diary notes, prices, currency, region, language and settings are saved only on your device (local storage). We do not receive or see this data. If you uninstall the app or clear its data, it is deleted. You can export a backup file yourself, and you decide where to keep it.

2. Information we do not collect

We do not collect your name, email, phone number, precise location, contacts, photos or payment information. The app does not contain advertising and does not sell or share personal data.

3. Optional features that use the internet

  • Buildexa AI: The built-in assistant works offline. If you choose to add your own API key to enable online AI, the questions you type are sent directly from your device to the AI provider (Anthropic) to get an answer. Your key is stored only on your device. The provider's own privacy policy applies to that request.
  • Weather: If you use the weather feature, the place name you choose is sent to a public weather service (wttr.in) to get the forecast.
  • Fonts and libraries: When you are online, the app may load fonts and code libraries from public content delivery networks (such as Google Fonts, cdnjs, jsDelivr and unpkg). These services may receive your IP address as part of a normal web request.
  • Sharing: When you share a result by WhatsApp or another app, or export a PDF or CSV, you choose what to send and who receives it.

4. Notifications

If you allow notifications, they are generated on your device (for example reminders). No notification data is sent to us.

5. Children

Buildexa is a general tool for builders and home owners and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

6. Data security and your choices

Because your data stays on your device, you control it. You can delete it at any time by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app. Estimates in the app are a guide only; please confirm quantities and prices with a professional before ordering materials.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.
